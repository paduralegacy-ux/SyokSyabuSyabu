Our Story

Why We're Syok

Syok Syabu Syabu started with one simple idea: hotpot night should feel effortless and always Syok. Every cut of meat, every sauce, and every condiment we sell is sourced exclusively from Halal-certified suppliers, prepped in small batches, and packed the same day it ships — so your pot tastes like it came straight from a proper steamboat restaurant, at home.

  1. 01

    Sourced from Halal Farms

    Every supplier is certified and audited before we work with them.

  2. 02

    Prepped Fresh Daily

    Sliced, portioned, and packed in small batches — never frozen for months.

  3. 03

    Delivered to Your Pot

    Chilled delivery straight to your door, ready when hotpot night is.
